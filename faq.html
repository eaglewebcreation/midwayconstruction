FAQ

Questions, answered.

Service area, estimates, and what we do. Still wondering about something? Call or text Pete.

Where are you based, and where do you work?

Our shop is at 1785 US-310 in Lovell, Wyoming. We take jobs in Lovell, Cowley, Byron, Deaver and around the Big Horn Basin.

How far will you travel for a job?

It depends on the job. Call or text Pete at 307-254-0578 with the location and he'll tell you straight whether it's a fit.

Are estimates free?

Yes. Estimates are free.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, Midway Construction & Dirtwork is licensed and insured.

Can you handle my whole project?

That's the point of us. Excavation and sub-grade, water, sewer and power lines, foundations and concrete, the shop itself, the driveway and the finish work, all with one crew.

Do you do stamped or decorative concrete?

Yes, including raised stamped patios.

Do you pour concrete in the winter?

We have poured in the cold and after dark when a job called for it. Pete will tell you what makes sense for your timing.

Do you build metal shops or wood shops?

Both, start to finish: foundation, building, electrical, insulation, heating and more.

Do you install in-ground trampolines?

Yes. We dig the hole, set the retaining wall and install the trampoline level with the lawn. See how it goes.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday.

When should I book a spring project?

The sooner the better. Call or text Pete at 307-254-0578 to get on the schedule.

How do I get an estimate?

Call or text 307-254-0578, email pete.midway@gmail.com, or send the details.
